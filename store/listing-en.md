```
Crossfour is a modern take on the classic Ludo board game that works completely offline. Play against computer players, pass one phone around with friends and family, or try the Lucky Chaos Ludo and Mystery Tiles modes, anywhere and without an internet connection.

NEW IN 1.5.5
• Arrow: jump +4 only on exact land (pass-over is normal). Dice faces stay visible after every roll.

NEW IN 1.5.4
• Board polish: richer texture and lighting, sharper lanes, clearer stars and arrows, deeper yards and home center (Midnight, Classic Wood, Graphite).

NEW IN 1.5.3

NEW IN 1.5.2
• Arrow fair play: forced rides never capture; arrow tiles are safe; trailing players leave base on a 5. Extra board polish.

NEW IN 1.5.1
• Arrow Ludo starts with 1 token already on the start square (no 6 wait for that token). Extra board and dice polish.

NEW IN 1.5
• Polished Ludo board (wood/felt texture, clearer lanes, safe stars and arrow tiles), stronger turn banner, capture/home celebrations, and a fresher sound pack.

NEW IN 1.3
• Quick Ludo (2 tokens, faster games) and Team Ludo (2v2, partners opposite, shared win).
• Arrow Ludo: one-way tiles, one extra step clockwise. Friendly: no captures, pure race. App stays 13+.
• Win screen shows moves and time. Free Midnight and Classic Wood boards. No purchases.

NEW IN 1.2: LUCKY CHAOS LUDO
• Normal Ludo plus luck you can steer. 4 Boost tiles spin the Boost wheel (Shield, Jump +3, Double Roll, Extra Roll, Lucky 6, Safe Escape) and 4 Chaos tiles spin the Chaos wheel (Bomb, Swap, Zap, Freeze, Back 3, Wild Jump).
• 4 Danger tiles: High Risk / High Reward. A 50/50 spin with stronger results.
• Lucky Streaks, a Revenge Charge after you are captured (pick 1 of 2 results) and a Lucky Charge meter: fill it to 5/5 and spin the Mega Wheel when you choose.
• King tokens: capture two rivals and your token wears a crown for 3 turns. Topple a King to charge your Mega Wheel.
• Store up to 2 powers, make quick choices, and enjoy fair protections near home. 1 v 1 or 4 players, vs computer or pass & play.

ALSO INSIDE
• Your own dice in your own corner, Star-style stacked 6s (three 6s lose the turn), Mystery Tiles mode, undo dice roll, quick chat and emotes, levels, XP and ranked results.

HOW TO PLAY
• Each player has 4 tokens. Roll a 6 to bring a token out of your base.
• Move clockwise around the board, then up your own coloured lane to the centre.
• You need the exact roll to reach home.
• Land on a rival token to capture it and send it back to its base. A capture gives you a bonus roll, and so does bringing a token home.
• Start squares and star squares are safe.
• The first player with all 4 tokens home wins.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• 1 v 1, 3 or 4 players in any mix of humans and computer players.
• Computer players in three levels: Easy for a relaxed game, Normal for a fair fight and Hard for a real challenge. Hard hunts for captures, stays safe, builds blocks and uses wheels, powers, the Mega Wheel and Kings cleverly.
• Pass & play: take turns on one phone with friends and family.
• House rules in Settings: Star style or Classic rolls (move each 6 before rolling again), safe squares, capture to enter home, blocks, and bonus rolls.
• A full rules screen and a How to play page explain every rule and every wheel result.

SMOOTH AND SATISFYING
• 3D dice, tokens that step square by square, capture bursts and animated event wheels.
• Crisp sound effects and light haptics (both can be turned off).
• Auto-move when only one move is possible, plus a fast animation mode.
• Your match is saved automatically. Leave any time and continue later.

SKINS AND STATS
• Earn coins and XP by finishing matches and unlock board skins (Walnut, Aurora) and dice skins (Onyx, Brass, Frost, Ember). Graphite and Linen boards are free.
• Coins are for cosmetic skins only. They cannot be bought, bet or cashed out, and no mode has an entry fee.
• Track your level, wins, captures, Lucky Chaos and Mystery Tiles wins, and wins against each computer level.

PLAY OFFLINE
Local play needs no data. Online Classic uses the same board as offline. Quick chat stays on your own screen.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Crossfour is free and shows ads. An extra dice-roll undo (after the 3 free ones) and "2x coins" after a match come from optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch.
• No in-app purchases, no betting and no real-money prizes. The wheels only change the board and never award or cost coins.
• Intended for players aged 13 and over.
• Privacy policy: https://offerpk.github.io/offline-ludo/privacy.html

Roll your dice and bring all four tokens home. Download Crossfour and play Ludo offline, your way.
```

## Claims check

Every claim matches the current game (v1.3.0):

- **Completely offline, no online mode, no account; quick chat is preset and local:** the app has no server code; only the AdMob SDK uses the network, and the game works without it. Chat bubbles are drawn locally.
- **Per-player dice in each corner, active glow, optional turn timer (off by default):** `www/js/game.js` (pods); positions checked by `test/browser.test.js`.
- **Star-style stacked rolls, chips, three 6s forfeit, capture/home bonus, 6 to leave, exact home, safe squares, Classic option, capture to enter, blocks:** `www/js/logic.js`, each unit-tested in `test/logic.test.js`; documented in `RULES.md` and the in-game Rules screen.
- **Mystery Tiles events (12) and wheels:** `applyEvent()` in `logic.js`; every event unit-tested; wheel checked in the browser test.
- **Lucky Chaos Ludo (12 tiles, Boost/Chaos/Danger wheels, streak, revenge, charge meter + Mega Wheel, King tokens, up to 2 stored powers, choices, near-home protections, 1 v 1 / 4 players, vs AI and pass & play):** `luckyActivate()`, `mega()`, `usePower()`, `choose()` in `logic.js`; every effect unit-tested in `test/lucky.test.js` with 1000-game balance simulations; wheels, Danger tile, choice window, Mega Wheel and King crown checked in the browser test.
- **Quick, Team, Arrow and Friendly modes:** `test/modes13.test.js` (300 games each, all finish).
- **Undo dice roll within ~2 s, 3 per match:** `undoRoll()`; browser-tested.
- **Three AI levels; Hard hunts captures, stays safe, builds blocks, uses events:** `chooseMove()`; tests check preferences and that Hard beats Easy/Normal in simulated games.
- **Skins with coins; coins can't be bought, bet or cashed out; no entry fee:** `www/js/themes.js`; no billing library; no mode takes coins to play.
- **Extra undo and 2x coins via optional rewarded ads:** only these two buttons call `Ads.showRewarded`.
- **13+:** matches the target audience answers and the privacy policy.
