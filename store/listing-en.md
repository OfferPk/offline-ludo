```
Crossfour is a modern take on the classic Ludo board game that works completely offline. Play against computer players, pass one phone around with friends and family, or mix both, anywhere and without an internet connection.

HOW TO PLAY
• Each player has 4 pieces. Roll a 6 to bring a piece out of your base.
• Move clockwise around the board, then up your own coloured lane to the centre.
• You need the exact roll to reach home.
• Land on an opponent to capture it and send it back to its base.
• Three 6s in a row lose the turn.
• The first player with all 4 pieces home wins.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• 2, 3 or 4 players in any mix of humans and computer players.
• Computer players in three levels: Easy for a relaxed game, Medium for a fair fight, and Hard for players who want a real challenge. Hard hunts for captures, uses safe squares, brings pieces home and avoids danger.
• Pass & play: take turns on one phone with friends and family.
• House rules you can switch on or off for every match: safe squares, extra turn on a 6, and extra turn on a capture.

SMOOTH AND SATISFYING
• A 3D dice roll and pieces that step square by square.
• Capture effects, a clean modern board and crisp sound effects.
• Light haptics (can be turned off).
• Auto-move when only one move is possible (can be turned off), plus a fast animation mode.
• Your match is saved automatically. Leave any time and continue later.

SKINS AND STATS
• Earn coins by finishing matches and unlock board skins (Walnut, Aurora) and dice skins (Onyx, Brass, Frost, Ember). Graphite and Linen boards are free.
• Coins are for cosmetic skins only. They cannot be bought, bet or cashed out.
• Track your matches, wins, captures and wins against each computer level.

PLAY OFFLINE
No Wi-Fi or mobile data needed. There is no online mode, no chat and no account, so it's perfect for flights, trips and family evenings.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Crossfour is free and shows ads. "Undo last move" (against computer players) and "2x coins" after a match come from optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch.
• No in-app purchases, no betting and no real-money prizes.
• Intended for players aged 13 and over.
• Privacy policy: https://offerpk.github.io/offline-ludo/privacy.html

Roll the die and bring all four pieces home. Download Crossfour and play Ludo offline, your way.
```

## Claims check

Every claim matches the current game (v1.0.0):

- **Completely offline, no online mode, no chat, no account:** the app has no server code at all; only the AdMob SDK uses the network, and the game works without it.
- **Rules (6 to leave base, clockwise then own lane, exact roll, capture to base, three 6s lose the turn):** `www/js/logic.js`, each rule unit-tested in `test/logic.test.js`.
- **2–4 players, any mix, three AI levels; Hard hunts captures, uses safe squares, brings pieces home, avoids danger:** `chooseMove()` / `evalHard()`; tests check each preference and that Hard beats Easy/Medium over 600-game samples.
- **House-rule toggles per match:** setup screen; toggles tested in both logic and browser tests.
- **3D dice roll, step-by-step moves, capture effects, sound, haptics, auto-move, fast mode, auto-save:** `www/js/game.js`, exercised by the headless Chrome test.
- **Skins with coins; Graphite and Linen free; coins can't be bought, bet or cashed out:** `www/js/themes.js`; there is no billing library in the app.
- **Undo (vs computer) and 2x coins via optional rewarded ads:** only these two buttons call `Ads.showRewarded`.
- **13+:** matches the target audience answers and the privacy policy.
