                Rules

                • 2–4 players, 4 tokens each. The first to bring all 4 tokens home to the centre wins; the others are ranked 2nd, 3rd and 4th.
                • Each player rolls their own die, shown next to their name in their corner.
                • Roll a 6 to bring a token out of base onto your start square.
                • Tokens move clockwise round the board, then up your own coloured lane. You need the exact roll to reach home.
                • Land on a rival token to capture it: it goes back to its base and you get a bonus roll.
                • A token reaching home also earns a bonus roll.
                • Safe squares: the start squares and the grey stars. Nobody can be captured there; tokens share the square.
                • Three 6s in a row cancel the whole turn, including any rolls not yet moved.
                • Star style (default): a 6 gives you another roll straight away. Your rolls stack up as chips next to your die, for example 663.
                • After your first roll that is not a 6, move your chips one by one, in any order. Tap a chip, then a token. When only one move is possible it is played for you.
                • Chips that can't be used (for example a 5 when every token is in base) are dropped.
                • Classic style: you move each roll before rolling again; a 6 still gives another roll after moving.
                • Undo dice roll: for about 2 seconds after your roll you can undo it and roll again. 3 free undos per match; after that, an optional ad gives one more. You can switch undo off in Settings.
                • Turn timer (optional, off by default): if time runs out the game rolls or moves for you.

                Mystery Tiles is a Crossfour mode with 8 special tiles on the track. End a move on one to spin its wheel. A tile rests for two rounds after it has been used. There's no entry fee or stake: every match is free and coins are cosmetic.

                Lucky Chaos Ludo: how to play. Normal Ludo rules (Star-style rolls and your house rules), plus luck you can steer. No entry fee, no stakes: every match is free and coins are cosmetic.

                • ? 4 Boost tiles and ! 4 Chaos tiles: end a move exactly on one to spin its wheel. A used tile rests for 2 rounds (the number on it shows the rounds left).
                • !! 4 Danger tiles, High Risk / High Reward: a 50/50 spin of the Boost or Chaos wheel with stronger results (Jump +5, 2 extra rolls, Big Bomb, Back 5).
                • Lucky Charge (5 pips under your name): +1 per wheel spin, +1 more on a Danger tile or when far behind, never more than +2 from one spin (tiles charge once per turn), and +1 per capture. At 5/5 tap MEGA before rolling to spin the Mega Wheel.
                • Lucky Streak (flame): each tile spin raises it (max ×3). Higher streaks give a chance of a better wheel result. It resets when one of your tokens is captured.
                • Revenge Charge: when your token is captured, your next tile spin shows two results and you pick one.
                • King token (crown): a token that captures 2 rival tokens becomes King for 3 of your turns: +1 square on moves when it fits and immune to Chaos effects. Capturing a King adds +2 Lucky Charge on top of the capture, not a full meter.
                • Stored powers (Double Roll, Lucky 6, Safe Escape): hold up to 2, never two of the same. Tap one next to your die before a roll; one power per roll.
                • Choices: Swap, Wild Jump and Revenge give you about 3 seconds to decide; if time runs out the best option is picked for you.
                • Fair play: safe squares, shields and Kings are protected; Bombs slide tokens back 3 (never to base); Zap on a token 40+ squares along only slides it back 6; nothing touches tokens in the home lane. Players who are far behind get slightly luckier spins and faster charge (capped).

                • Quick Ludo. Two tokens each, same board and the same rules: a 6 to leave base, the exact roll to get home, safe squares. 1 v 1 or more, vs computer or pass & play. Matches end much faster.
                • Team Ludo, 2v2. All four corners play. Partners sit opposite: Coral with Saffron, Jade with Cobalt. A team wins when both partners have every token home. You cannot capture your partner, and a partner's block does not stop you. Pass & play, or you plus a computer partner against two computers.
                • Arrow Ludo. Each player starts with 1 token already on their start square — no 6 needed for that token (others still need a 6). Amber arrows on board number 4 of each side. Only if your move ends exactly on an arrow does the token jump 4 squares forward and stop (rivals on that jump path — and an unsafe stop — are captured). Passing over an arrow with a higher roll is normal movement with no jump. No chaining. Safe squares still protect. House-rule “Arrow tiles” use the same rule.
                • Friendly. Captures are off. It is a pure race to home. Safe for a calmer game. The app as a whole stays 13+.
                • No mode has an entry fee, a stake or a bet. Coins stay cosmetic.
                • House rules are in Settings and apply to new matches.
                • Roll style: Star (stacked rolls) or Classic (move each roll first).
                • Safe squares: switch off for a rougher game where any square can be hit.
                • Capture to enter home: you must capture at least once before your tokens may enter your home lane; until then they keep circling.
                • Blocks: two of your tokens on one square form a block. Rivals can't land on it or pass it.
                • Bonus roll on capture and on reaching home can each be switched off.
