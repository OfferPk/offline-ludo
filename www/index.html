    How to play

    • Each player has 4 pieces in a base. The first to bring all 4 home, to the centre, wins.
    • Roll a 6 to bring a piece out onto your start square.
    • Pieces move clockwise around the board, then up your own coloured lane to the centre. You need the exact roll to reach home.
    • Land on an opponent's piece to capture it: it goes back to its base.
    • Safe squares (stars and start squares) protect pieces from capture, if the rule is on.
    • A 6 and a capture earn an extra roll (both can be switched off). Three 6s in a row lose the turn.
    • When only one move is possible it is played for you (you can turn this off in Settings).
